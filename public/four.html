Mode

Standard puts every king on a light square (Red h1, Yellow g14, Blue a8, Green n7), each facing the opposing queen. Omatamix swaps Blue's and Green's king and queen.
Promotion: off = chess.com's rule: an FFA pawn becomes a queen automatically. Teams always lets you choose. A promoted piece is worth 1 point when captured. Applies to the next new game.

Seats

Several humans can share one screen: the board turns to whoever is up, or stays put if you pick a fixed view.

Display

Engine hints

The engine is the same alpha-beta search the bots use, run for about 2.5 s a turn. It assumes everyone not on your side plays against you.

How it plays
  • Order: Red → Blue → Yellow → Green, clockwise. Teams: Red + Yellow vs Blue + Green (teammates sit opposite).
  • Pawns advance toward the opposite side and promote to a queen on the 8th rank (FFA) or the 11th (Teams). Castling is allowed; there is no en passant.
  • Check comes from any opponent. Kings are never captured: you are checkmated when your turn comes and you cannot escape. In FFA the mated player is out and their pieces turn grey and dead.
  • FFA points: pawn 1, knight 3, bishop 5, rook 5, queen 9 (a promoted queen is worth 1), checkmate +20, stalemate +20 (to the stalemated player), forking two or three kings +1/+5 (queen) or +5/+20. Three eliminations end the game; most points wins.
  • Teams: the first checkmate wins; stalemate is a draw.
  • Draws: threefold repetition, 50 rounds without a capture or pawn move, or too little material (FFA: +10 each).
